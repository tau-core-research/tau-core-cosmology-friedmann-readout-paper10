Description

Complete Pantheon+ compilation of 1701 Type Ia supernovae with standardized distance measurements. Includes redshifts, apparent magnitudes, distance moduli, and systematic uncertainties. 
 
Used for testing UDT distance relations d = z × R₀.
 
Original source: https://github.com/PantheonPlusSH0ES/DataRelease and https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2022ApJ...938..110S
